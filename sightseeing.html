Experience Berchtesgaden in private - with your exclusive Multivan tour from Mountain Concierge!

Just imagine: Your personal driver welcomes you directly at your desired destination and you take a seat in a luxurious Multivan. Whether hotel, vacation apartment or train station - we pick you up, discuss your individual wishes together and your tailor-made adventure begins.

Your route - as flexible as your interests

Whether you want to experience the majestic nature around the emerald-green Königssee and the legendary Kehlsteinhaus, take a boat trip to the famous pilgrimage church of St. Bartholomä or enjoy breathtaking Alpine views on the Rossfeld panoramic road - everything is possible. For hiking enthusiasts, the spectacular gorges of the Wimbachklamm or Almbachklamm gorges are ideal.

Those interested in history and culture will get their money's worth when visiting the 500-year-old salt mine, the Obersalzberg documentation center or the imposing Berchtesgaden Castle. Or stroll through the charming old town, enjoy regional specialties in traditional cafés and discover small villages such as Ramsau or Schönau.

Your comfort is our focus

Sit back and enjoy the ride in a modern Multivan with comfortable seats, air conditioning, generous space and, if you wish, snacks, drinks as well as WiFi and multimedia for background information or your favorite music.

Why Mountain Concierge?

We offer you the opportunity to discover the sights in and around Berchtesgaden privately and in peace, without the hustle and bustle of mass tourism. Benefit from our local expertise and experience the mountains from a whole new perspective.
