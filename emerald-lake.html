Natural Wonders & Historic Charm

Your private mountain lake tour is more than just a boat ride; it is a customized experience, perfectly tailored to your wishes and the current weather. Dive into the fascinating beauty of one of the most stunning lakes in the Alps.

Journey & description

  1. Start: Pickup & Welcome

    Your local guide meets you at the arranged time at the mountain lake.

  2. First Stage: Onto the Lake

    After boarding your private boat, you glide across the emerald waters of the alpine lake. Majestic mountain slopes and pristine, clear water create a picturesque setting. Enjoy the relaxing ride as your guide shares fascinating stories and facts about the region.

  3. Experience the Famous Echo

    A highlight of the tour is the famous echo of the alpine lake. Take a moment to call out and hear your voice echo off the impressive cliffs—a magical phenomenon and perfect photo opportunity.

  4. Visit the Pilgrimage Church Pilgrimage Church The famous church on a peninsula, with iconic red domes. See details

    The first stop is a charming peninsula where you can visit the distinctive pilgrimage church with onion domes. This idyllic spot, surrounded by mountains, offers countless photo opportunities and a tranquil lakeside walk.

  5. Optional Exploration: Hidden Lake Hidden Lake A secluded mountain lake with crystal-clear water and an impressive backdrop. See details

    On request, your guide can lead you to a beautiful hidden lake. Here, you’ll find breathtaking scenery, clear waters, and stunning mountains. Take a peaceful walk along the shore or simply enjoy the serenity of nature.

  6. Relaxed Finish: Return & Refreshments

    On the return trip, relax with a cool drink or a warm refreshment and reflect on the experiences of the day. Your guide is always available for any questions or extra information.

This private lake tour gives you the chance to discover unspoiled nature and the fascinating culture of the region in a personal setting—an unforgettable adventure for all the senses!
