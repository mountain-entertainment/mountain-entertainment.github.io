Private Winter Wonder Tour: Berchtesgaden Alps & Advent Magic

Your private tour is more than just a ride, it is a tailor-made experience that adapts flexibly to weather conditions and your personal wishes.

Your vehicle: A comfortable, off-road premium SUV or van with four-wheel drive, driven by a knowledgeable local driver-guide who will provide you with exciting stories and insider tips.

Journey & description

  1. Start: Pick-up & getting in the mood

    Your guide will pick you up from your accommodation at an agreed time. Over a cup of hot tea or coffee, you will briefly discuss the day's itinerary. The route will be planned optimally depending on the current weather and road conditions.

  2. Summit experience: Alpine Loop Alpine Loop Follow the winding road high above the clouds – where every curve reveals a new, breathtaking view of the Alps. See more

    This private toll road, which is only partially passable in winter, is the absolute highlight of the tour.

    With snow: A breathtaking drive into a cloud-covered winter wonderland. The road winds its way up to an altitude of 1,600 meters and offers spectacular views of snow-covered fir trees, bizarre ice formations, and the mighty peaks of the Berchtesgaden Alps rising out of the sea of fog. A stop at one of the viewing platforms is a must for breathtaking photos.

    Without snow: The entire road is often passable. You can enjoy clear, sharp views all the way to Salzburg and the entire Alpine chain. The colors are intense—the deep green of the fir trees, the white of the peaks, and the blue of the sky. The drive is just as spectacular, but with a completely different, clear drama.

  3. Stillness & mysticism: The rustic lake Enchanted Forest & Emerald lake Join us on a visit to the rustic mountain lake in the valley and the enchanted forest See more in the Valley

    We continue to a picturesque lake, an emerald green mountain lake at the foot of the Hochkalter massif.

    With snow: The lake lies absolutely still and peaceful, often partially frozen and surrounded by a powdery blanket of snow. The silence is broken only by the crunch of your footsteps. The famous enchanted forest is transformed into a fairytale ice landscape with snow-covered, moss-covered rocks and bizarre ice formations on the trees. A short winter walk here is magical.

    Without snow: The lake perfectly reflects the surrounding steep rock faces. A walk around the lake or through the enchanted forest Enchanted Forest & Emerald lake Join us on a visit to the rustic mountain lake in the valley and the enchanted forest See more on easily accessible paths is wonderfully refreshing, and the air is crystal clear.

  4. Alpine view: Peaks (4,387 ft) Skyline peaks Breathtaking 360° view of mountains, lake, and valley – an unforgettable natural experience See more

    Then you drive to the mountain station of a cable car. The ride up in the gondola is an experience in itself.

    From above, whatever the weather: nce you reach the top, you can enjoy one of the most panoramic views in the region. You look directly onto the Watzmann, Königssee, and Berchtesgaden Valley. On the sun terrace of the mountain restaurant, you can enjoy this view with a cup of mulled wine or a warm snack (e.g., a “Brotzeitplatte” snack platter). The contrast between the warm interior and the cold, majestic mountain world is unforgettable.

  5. Cosy finale: The Adventsmarkt Berchtesgaden
