Welcome to Mountain Concierge

About us:

Mountain Concierge is a new type of agency that does not yet exist in mountain regions. We are at your side as your local guides to introduce you to the breathtaking nature and culture of the Berchtesgaden valley basin. Our tours are as individual as you are and offer you the opportunity to experience the beauty of the Alps in a private and stylish way..

Our tours:

Discover the variety of our tours, which are perfectly tailored to your wishes:

  • Insider Tour: Discover hidden stories, legends, and regional secrets with our local guides as you explore the breathtaking landscape.
  • Photostop Tour: Capture the best moments! Our guides will show you the most stunning photo opportunities in the area.
  • Sightseeing Tour: Experience Berchtesgaden’s highlights and hidden gems in comfort, tailored to your interests.
  • Custom Tour: Design your own private journey according to your wishes—fully personal and exclusive.

...and many more!

All private tours currently take place in the picturesque valley valley of Berchtesgaden. Our comfortable VW buses and the VW Amarok will take you to the most beautiful places in the region in style. We provide you with plenty of water on every tour to keep you refreshed.

Why Mountain Concierge?

We offer you the opportunity to discover the sights in and around Berchtesgaden privately and in peace, without the hustle and bustle of mass tourism. Benefit from our local expertise and experience the mountains from a whole new perspective.
