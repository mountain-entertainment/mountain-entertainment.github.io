Private photo stop sightseeing tour in a Multivan - your exclusive experience in Berchtesgaden

Just imagine: You glide relaxed in a comfortable Multivan through the majestic mountain world around Berchtesgaden, stopping where nature is at its most impressive and capturing unforgettable moments with your camera. This is exactly what our private photo stop sightseeing tour offers you - individual, flexible and tailored to your wishes.

Your day, your route - tailor-made and stress-free

Enjoy a day just the way you want it - tailor-made, relaxed and full of special moments. Your personal driver and guide will pick you up directly from your hotel, train station or a location of your choice and work with you to create your own individual route. Whether you want to make spontaneous photo stops or opt for extensive sightseeing: Everything is possible, because you set the pace. This allows you to experience the most beautiful places in the region without any hustle and bustle and far away from mass tourism - just you and the impressive natural backdrop of Berchtesgaden.

Highlights that inspire

  • Königssee: Mirror-smooth water, framed by steep rock faces - a picture-book photo motif. Optional: boat trip to the famous St. Bartholomä church.
  • Obersalzberg & Eagle's Nest: Breathtaking panoramic views of the Alps, history at your fingertips
  • Rossfeld Panorama Road: One of the most spectacular high roads in the Alps - the perfect backdrop for unforgettable photos.
  • Hintersee & Magic Forest: Mystical forests, crystal-clear lakes - nature that enchants.
  • Dürnberg Salt Mine: An exciting stopover for families and explorers.
  • Optional: A detour to charming Salzburg - baroque splendor, winding alleyways and the Hohensalzburg Fortress await you.

Travel in the comfortable Multivan, which impresses with plenty of space for up to five people, comfortable seats and modern equipment. Enjoy every minute of your tour, as spontaneous requests and individual stops can be made at any time. The Multivan is exclusively available to your group - for maximum privacy and an all-round relaxed experience.
