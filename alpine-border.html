Gondola tour: An unforgettable mountain adventure

Your private gondola tour to the majestic alpine border is more than just a view; it is a tailor-made experience that is tailored to your wishes and the weather conditions.

Journey & description

  1. Start: Individual meeting point & welcome

    At an agreed time, you will meet your personal guide at a location of your choice.

  2. Gondola ride: 360-degree view

    You then board the private gondola, which has been in operation since 1961 and transports you to another world. Enjoy the spectacular ride with breathtaking 360-degree views of the surrounding Alps, including Berchtesgaden and the Salzburg region. Every meter brings you closer to the snow-capped peaks and green valleys, and the view is simply breathtaking.

  3. Mountain air & summit cross: Unmistakable views

    Although the hike to the summit cross takes about 45 minutes there and back, you can decide for yourself whether you want to make this climb before or after lunch. The trail takes you past impressive rocks and delicate alpine flowers. From the summit cross, you can enjoy the magnificent view of the Watzmann and the surrounding mountain landscape. Up here, you feel close to nature and can experience the profound silence of the mountains.

  4. Private lunch: Culinary delights high above

    After or before your summit climb, a very special highlight awaits you: a private lunch at the mountain station, where you will be served various regional delicacies. Indulge in local specialties and enjoy the beautiful view while you eat. The combination of delicious food and impressive scenery makes this an unforgettable experience.

  5. All-round enjoyment: farewell to the heights

    Following unforgettable hours on the alpine border you will embark on the return journey in your private gondola with many new impressions. The entire tour lasts up to three hours and ends at your chosen meeting point. Your guide will ensure that you not only experience the beauty of nature, but also take home the comfort and warmth of an unforgettable excursion.

Why Mountain Concierge?

We offer you the opportunity to discover the sights in and around Berchtesgaden privately and in peace, without the hustle and bustle of mass tourism. Benefit from our local expertise and experience the mountains from a whole new perspective.
